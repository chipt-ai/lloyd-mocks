FAQ

Questions before you begin.

What is GLP-1?

GLP-1 medications are prescription medications that may be used as part of medically-supervised weight management. A licensed provider can help determine whether this type of treatment may be appropriate for you.

Is it safe?

Like any prescription medication, GLP-1 medications can have side effects and are not appropriate for everyone. Your provider reviews your health history and discusses relevant considerations before prescribing when appropriate.

Do I qualify?

Eligibility depends on your health history, current medications, goals, and provider review. The online assessment is designed to help a licensed provider evaluate whether treatment may be appropriate.

How fast does it ship?

If prescribed, timing can vary based on provider review, pharmacy processing, and shipping logistics. GLP Wellness coordinates discreet shipping after the prescription process is complete.

Is my info private?

Your information is handled with privacy in mind and used to support your online evaluation, provider review, pharmacy coordination, and ongoing care.
